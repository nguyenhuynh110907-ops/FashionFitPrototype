Read the current source/player manifests and fresh suite summary. Evaluate input,
material, appearance, performance and physical-HMD scope separately. Compare
screenshots for visual changes; pure topology checks cannot judge aesthetics.
For contact reports verify original unmasked bodies, export hashes, states and
sampling limitations. For material claims require units and measured calibration.
Mark absent evidence NOT_ASSESSED; do not grant a clean release on empty scope.
Return concrete bugs, exact evidence paths and uncovered controller sequences.
Do not rewrite gameplay, add dependencies or publish during a read-only review.
