Technical_Female_Body.obj: CC0 MakeHuman-derived temporary female body. Not the user's original model; not a garment.

Try: import skirt > choose units > review dimensions and waist/hip > confirm > wear copy.
Body target waist Y=1.035 m and hip Y=0.89 m belong ONLY to the temporary mannequin.
The sample skirt source top is Y=1.045 m, hip approximately Y=0.89 m.
The original-size mode preserves all dimensions after unit conversion and translation.
Adapt mode is an initial geometric preview, NOT validated clothing fit or physics for imported OBJ.
Textures/MTL are not automatically loaded; use the separate base-color / RGB +Y normal-map controls.
